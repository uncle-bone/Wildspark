## WildFire Kit 1.10

**New: Hide while you have Camp Benefits**
- A setting under General's Show / hide (off to start): while you already have Camp Benefits, the tent, its pinned slots and its timers fade out, like Only show near a campfire.
- Show again when Camp Benefits is under: pick 5 to 60 minutes and the kit comes back once the buff has less than that left, so you can top it up. Off: it stays hidden until the buff is gone.
- Works alongside Only show near a campfire: with both on, the kit shows only at a fire and only while you need the buff. Opening the kit (key binding or the minimap button) still brings it back until you close it.

**New: announcing options**
- A new Announcing section in the settings (the announce button's setting moved there from At a camp).
- Post in: tick any of General chat (on to start), Say, Yell, Party or raid, and Guild; one click posts to each. Ones you can't post to right now (no group, no guild) are skipped, and the kit says which.
- Use your own message: type it in a box right in the settings (up to 150 letters), with buttons that add {fire}, {pin}, {count}, {objects} and {looking}, filled in when it's sent, and a preview under it as you type. If it comes out too long for chat, what's at the camp is shortened. Left empty, the kit's own message is sent.
- Kit Core v23 adds the text box to the settings window. With an older Kit Core loaded by another kit, an Edit... button opens a small editor instead.
- Right-click the chat bubble, or the Announce button in This campfire..., to open the settings at Announcing. Their tooltips say where the camp will be posted.

## WildFire Kit 1.9.1

**Fixes**
- The reminder panel's box no longer blocks clicks on whatever is under it. It only takes the mouse while you're moving it; the reminder buttons in it still work as before (Kit Core v22).

## WildFire Kit 1.9

**New: a settings window of its own**
- Settings open in the kit's own window instead of the game's options list: the kits' look (dark panel, gold edge), its sections down the left in groups (Kit, WildFire Kit, Camps), and each setting with its description under it.
- Sections are split into small groups with their own headings (General: Show / hide, Combat; Reminders: Campfire, Well Fed, Weapons; Camps on the map: where camps come from, where they show).
- Moved so they're easier to find: the profession buttons, the Camp Benefits and Well Fed timers and Only show near a campfire are under General's Show / hide; Slots is now Pinned buttons; Flag camp objects your buffs would cancel is under At a camp.
- Look's sizes say what they do: Overall size (the whole kit), Center icon size (was Kit button size), a new Button size for the buttons round the tent on their own, and Distance from center for how far they sit from it.
- Open it from the minimap button, the tent's right-click menu, or `/ck`. Esc closes it.
- The kit's settings are only in this window now: under Esc > Options > AddOns the kit just has a button that opens it.

**New: Only show near a campfire**
- A setting under General's Show / hide (off to start): away from a fire, the tent, its pinned slots and its timers fade out, and they fade back in when you reach one.
- Opening the kit (key binding or the minimap button) brings it back until you close it.
- On the Kit Dock pin or in a box the kit steps out while it's hidden, and the other kits close up.

**New: more in your pick slots**
- Firestones and Spellstones count as weapon buffs: a your-pick slot holding one glows while your main-hand weapon doesn't have it on.
- New Your pick slots settings let the slots take potions, elixirs, flasks, scrolls, bandages, and engineering explosives and devices. All off to start.

**New: Kit Core's reminder panel**
- Kit Core v21 adds a reminder panel shared by every kit: one panel you can drag anywhere. In this kit's settings you choose whether it uses the panel and which of its reminders go there.
- With the kit closed, those reminders show in the panel as full-size copies that glow, count down and cast like the slot, instead of beside the tent. Open the kit and they're back in their places. Pinned buttons always stay by the kit.
- The Sit button is the exception: it only matters at the fire, so it always stays by the tent and never goes to the panel.
- Kit Core v21 also makes the scroll bar in the settings window draggable (click the track to jump).

**Fixes**
- The minimap button now works with square minimaps and minimap button-bag addons (Leatrix Plus and the like). It stays where you had it.
