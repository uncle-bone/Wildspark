# Forever Minimap Target attribution

`TargetTracking.lua` adapts and modifies Forever Minimap Target v1.0.1 by Ashtar Senior.
The upstream feature restores Blizzard's Target and Focus filters in the minimap tracking menu and reapplies per-character preferences after loading screens. WildSpark adds WildMaps-tab controls and stores these choices with WildSpark's character data.

Upstream package: `ForeverMinimapTarget-v1.0.1-forever.zip` (provided by the user).
