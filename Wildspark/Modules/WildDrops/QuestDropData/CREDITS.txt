Data
----

Data.lua (Libs/QuestDropData)
    Quests, required items, the creatures that drop them, their English names
    and spawn points per zone: generated from QuestieDB
    (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), GPL-3.0 license, WoW Forever data.
    Drop rates: WoW Forever drop data and the kills and loot recorded by
    QuestLuck players.
    Item categories ("Quest Item", item class): WoW Forever client tables
    ItemSparse and Item, read from https://wago.tools.
