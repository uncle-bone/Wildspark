Changes made for this WildInfo snapshot: game-object sources were excluded;
the API's rate fraction was converted to a percentage; records were keyed by
NPC and item ID and serialized as Lua. Sample counts, reporter counts, and
confidence tiers are retained. The snapshot date is in CrowdDropRates.lua.
